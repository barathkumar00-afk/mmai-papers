# Normal Brain Neuroanatomy, Differential Diagnostic Framework and Pseudolesions

PROVENANCE: Local reference note authored for the MedExplain AI research prototype.
Ground truth for multimodal brain disease clinical decision support. Citable within system.

## Normal Brain MRI Morphology (Negative Control Parameters)
1. **Symmetry & Ventricular Configuration**:
   - Lateral ventricles symmetrical in volume, morphology, and fronto-occipital horns without effacement, hydrocephalus, or dilation.
   - Third and fourth ventricles in normal midline anatomical alignment without lateral displacement or downward transtentorial pressure.
   - Symmetrical hemispheric sulcation and cortical thickness appropriate for patient age.
2. **Gray-White Matter Junction**:
   - Preserved demarcation between deep cortical ribbon (gray matter) and subcortical white matter without blurring, loss of contrast, or focal dedifferentiation.
3. **Midline Structures**:
   - Preserved midline interhemispheric falx cerebri with 0.0 mm midline shift.
   - Optic chiasm, pituitary gland in sella turcica, brainstem (midbrain, pons, medulla), and cerebellar vermis normal in volume and signal characteristics.
4. **Signal Intensity Baselines**:
   - Normal physiological CSF is homogenously dark (suppressed) on T2/FLAIR and bright on standard T2.
   - Absence of pathological restricted diffusion on DWI (normal b=1000 and corresponding ADC map values 700-800 x 10^-6 mm^2/s).
   - Absence of abnormal leptomeningeal, pachymeningeal, intra-axial nodular, or ring enhancement following intravenous gadolinium.

## Differential Diagnostic Framework for Intracranial Mass Lesions
When an intracranial lesion or signal abnormality is identified, multiparametric evaluation distinguishes between primary neoplastic and non-neoplastic mimics:
- **Intra-Axial vs Extra-Axial**:
  - Intra-axial: Glioma, metastasis, primary CNS lymphoma, cerebral abscess, acute cerebral infarction, tumefactive demyelination.
  - Extra-axial: Meningioma, schwannoma, epidermoid cyst, pituitary macroadenoma, arachnoid cyst.
- **Ring Enhancement Differential (MAGIC DR mnemonic)**:
  - **M**: Metastasis (well-circumscribed, profound vasogenic edema out of proportion to tumor volume, gray-white junction).
  - **A**: Abscess (smooth thin rim, dual-rim sign on T2, central restricted diffusion on DWI with low ADC).
  - **G**: Glioblastoma (irregular, nodular, thick ragged ring with central necrosis and infiltrative FLAIR rim).
  - **I**: Infarct (subacute luxury perfusion, gyriform enhancement conforming to vascular territory).
  - **C**: Contusion / Hematoma (evolution of hemoglobin breakdown products, hemosiderin blooming on SWI/GRE).
  - **D**: Demyelinating lesion / Tumefactive MS (open-ring or incomplete horseshoe enhancement facing the cortex).
  - **R**: Radiation necrosis (soap-bubble or Swiss-cheese enhancement in previously irradiated field).

## Multimodal Decision Support Recommendations
1. For confirmed normal brain MRI scans in symptomatic patients (headache, dizziness, transient ischemic attacks), investigate metabolic, cervical, vestibular, vascular, or functional etiologies.
2. Consider dynamic MR Angiography (MRA) or CT Angiography (CTA) if acute neurovascular symptoms persist despite normal parenchymal MRI.
3. Schedule 3- to 6-month surveillance imaging if clinical suspicion for early or microscopic epileptogenic focus remains elevated.
